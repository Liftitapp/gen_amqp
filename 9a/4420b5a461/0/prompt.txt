### Cron Expression Examples

The user's local timezone is **America/Bogota**. Cron expressions and `run_once_at` timestamps are always in UTC. When the user says a local time, convert it to UTC but confirm with them: "9am America/Bogota = Xam UTC, so the cron would be `0 X * * 1-5`." For one-time runs, the same conversion applies — "run this at 3pm" → `"run_once_at": "YYYY-MM-DDTHH:00:00Z"` with their 3pm converted to UTC.

- `0 9 * * 1-5` — Every weekday at 9am **UTC**
- `0 */2 * * *` — Every 2 hours
- `0 0 * * *` — Daily at midnight **UTC**
- `30 14 * * 1` — Every Monday at 2:30pm **UTC**
- `0 8 1 * *` — First of every month at 8am **UTC**

Minimum interval is 1 hour. `*/30 * * * *` will be rejected.
